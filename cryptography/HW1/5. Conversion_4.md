# Hexadecimal to Binary and Decimal Conversion

Convert:

$$
(3A)_{16}
$$

to binary and decimal.

## 1. Hexadecimal to Binary

Each hexadecimal digit corresponds to **4 binary bits**.

* \(3_{16} = 0011_2\)
* \(A_{16} = 1010_2\)

Therefore:

$$
(3A)_{16} = (0011\ 1010)_2
$$

So:

$$
\boxed{(3A)_{16} = (00111010)_2}
$$

---

## 2. Hexadecimal to Decimal

Use the powers of 16:

$$
(3A)_{16} = 3 \times 16^1 + A \times 16^0
$$

Since \(A = 10\):

$$
= 3 \times 16 + 10 \times 1
$$

$$
= 48 + 10
$$

$$
= 58
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(3A)_{16} = (58)_{10}}
$$

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{(3A)_{16} = (00111010)_2 = (58)_{10}}
$$
