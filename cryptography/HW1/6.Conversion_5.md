# Complete Number Representations

Convert:

$$
(172)_{10} = (?)_2 = (?)_{16}
$$

## 1. Decimal to Binary

Use powers of 2:

$$
2^7=128,\quad 2^6=64,\quad 2^5=32,\quad 2^4=16,\quad
2^3=8,\quad 2^2=4,\quad 2^1=2,\quad 2^0=1
$$

We need to represent 172:

$$
172 = 128 + 32 + 8 + 4
$$

Now write **1** for the powers we used and **0** for the others:

| Power | \(2^7\) | \(2^6\) | \(2^5\) | \(2^4\) | \(2^3\) | \(2^2\) | \(2^1\) | \(2^0\) |
| ----- | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: |
| Value |     128 |      64 |      32 |      16 |       8 |       4 |       2 |       1 |
| Bit   |       1 |       0 |       1 |       0 |       1 |       1 |       0 |       0 |

Therefore:

$$
(172)_{10} = (10101100)_2
$$

---

## 2. Binary to Hexadecimal

Split the binary number into groups of 4 bits:

$$
1010\quad1100
$$

Convert each group:

$$
1010_2 = 10 = A_{16}
$$

$$
1100_2 = 12 = C_{16}
$$

Therefore:

$$
(10101100)_2 = (AC)_{16}
$$

---

## Final Answer

$$
\boxed{(172)_{10} = (10101100)_2 = (AC)_{16}}
$$

### Check

$$
A \times 16 + C = 10 \times 16 + 12 = 160 + 12 = 172
$$

So the conversion is correct.
