# Decimal to Binary Conversion Using Powers of 2

To convert a decimal number to binary, write the powers of 2 and determine which ones are needed to make the given number.

The positions are:

```text
2⁷  2⁶  2⁵  2⁴  2³  2²  2¹  2⁰
128  64   32   16    8    4    2    1
```

If a power of 2 is used, write `1`.
If it is not used, write `0`.

---

## a) \(13_{10}\)

Find powers of 2 that add up to 13:

$$
13 = 8 + 4 + 1
$$

Since:

$$
8=2^3,\quad 4=2^2,\quad 1=2^0
$$

we have:

```text
2³  2²  2¹  2⁰
 1   1   0   1
```

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(13)_{10}=(1101)_2}
$$

---

## b) \(27_{10}\)

Find powers of 2 that add up to 27:

$$
27 = 16 + 8 + 2 + 1
$$

Since:

$$
16=2^4,\quad 8=2^3,\quad 2=2^1,\quad 1=2^0
$$

we get:

```text
2⁴  2³  2²  2¹  2⁰
 1   1   0   1   1
```

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(27)_{10}=(11011)_2}
$$

---

## c) \(45_{10}\)

Find powers of 2 that add up to 45:

$$
45 = 32 + 8 + 4 + 1
$$

Since:

$$
32=2^5,\quad 8=2^3,\quad 4=2^2,\quad 1=2^0
$$

we get:

```text
2⁵  2⁴  2³  2²  2¹  2⁰
 1   0   1   1   0   1
```

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(45)_{10}=(101101)_2}
$$

---

## d) \(100_{10}\)

Find powers of 2 that add up to 100:

$$
100 = 64 + 32 + 4
$$

Since:

$$
64=2^6,\quad 32=2^5,\quad 4=2^2
$$

we get:

```text
2⁶  2⁵  2⁴  2³  2²  2¹  2⁰
 1   1   0   0   1   0   0
```

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(100)_{10}=(1100100)_2}
$$

---

## Final Answers

| Decimal | Powers of 2  | Binary    |
| ------: | ------------ | --------- |
|  \(13\) | \(8+4+1\)    | `1101`    |
|  \(27\) | \(16+8+2+1\) | `11011`   |
|  \(45\) | \(32+8+4+1\) | `101101`  |
| \(100\) | \(64+32+4\)  | `1100100` |

### Quick idea

For example:

$$
45=32+8+4+1
$$

So place `1` under 32, 8, 4, and 1, and `0` under the powers that are not used:

```text
32  16  8  4  2  1
 1   0  1  1  0  1
```

Therefore:

$$
45_{10}=101101_2
$$
