# ASCII Encoding and Decoding

Use the ASCII code of each character. For binary, use **8-bit representation**.

---

## 1. Encode `CAT` in ASCII and hexadecimal

First, find the ASCII code of each character:

| Character | ASCII Decimal | Hexadecimal |
| --------- | ------------: | ----------: |
| C         |            67 |          43 |
| A         |            65 |          41 |
| T         |            84 |          54 |

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{CAT \rightarrow (43\ 41\ 54)_{16}}
$$

### Check

* C = 67 = \(4 \times 16 + 3 = 43_{16}\)
* A = 65 = \(4 \times 16 + 1 = 41_{16}\)
* T = 84 = \(5 \times 16 + 4 = 54_{16}\)

---

## 2. Encode `KEY` in ASCII and 8-bit binary

### K

ASCII decimal value:

$$
K = 75
$$

Convert 75 to binary:

$$
75 = 64 + 8 + 2 + 1
$$

$$
75 = 2^6 + 2^3 + 2^1 + 2^0
$$

Therefore:

$$
75_{10} = 01001011_2
$$

### E

ASCII decimal value:

$$
E = 69
$$

$$
69 = 64 + 4 + 1
$$

Therefore:

$$
69_{10} = 01000101_2
$$

### Y

ASCII decimal value:

$$
Y = 89
$$

$$
89 = 64 + 16 + 8 + 1
$$

Therefore:

$$
89_{10} = 01011001_2
$$

So:

$$
\boxed{KEY \rightarrow (01001011,\ 01000101,\ 01011001)_2}
$$

# 4. Decode the hexadecimal ASCII message

Given:

$$
43\ 52\ 59\ 50\ 54\ 4F
$$

First convert each hexadecimal value to binary.

| Hex | Binary   | Decimal | ASCII |
| --- | -------- | ------: | ----- |
| 43  | 01000011 |      67 | C     |
| 52  | 01010010 |      82 | R     |
| 59  | 01011001 |      89 | Y     |
| 50  | 01010000 |      80 | P     |
| 54  | 01010100 |      84 | T     |
| 4F  | 01001111 |      79 | O     |

### Hexadecimal → Binary

$$
43_{16} = 01000011_2
$$

$$
52_{16} = 01010010_2
$$

$$
59_{16} = 01011001_2
$$

$$
50_{16} = 01010000_2
$$

$$
54_{16} = 01010100_2
$$

$$
4F_{16} = 01001111_2
$$

Therefore:

$$
43\ 52\ 59\ 50\ 54\ 4F
$$

becomes:

$$
01000011\ 01010010\ 01011001\ 01010000\ 01010100\ 01001111
$$

Now decode each 8-bit binary value using ASCII:

$$
01000011 = C
$$

$$
01010010 = R
$$

$$
01011001 = Y
$$

$$
01010000 = P
$$

$$
01010100 = T
$$

$$
01001111 = O
$$

So the resulting ASCII message is:

$$
\boxed{\text{CRYPTO}}
$$
