# Decimal to Hexadecimal Conversion

To convert a decimal number to hexadecimal, divide the number by **16**.

The remainders are converted to hexadecimal digits:

* 10 = A
* 11 = B
* 12 = C
* 13 = D
* 14 = E
* 15 = F

## 1. \((29)_{10}\)

$$
29 \div 16 = 1 \text{ remainder } 13
$$

13 in hexadecimal is **D**.

Therefore:

$$
(29)_{10} = (1D)_{16}
$$

---

## 2. \((156)_{10}\)

$$
156 \div 16 = 9 \text{ remainder } 12
$$

12 in hexadecimal is **C**.

Therefore:

$$
(156)_{10} = (9C)_{16}
$$

---

## 3. \((255)_{10}\)

$$
255 \div 16 = 15 \text{ remainder } 15
$$

15 in hexadecimal is **F**.

Therefore:

$$
(255)_{10} = (FF)_{16}
$$

## Final Answers

|      Decimal | Hexadecimal |
| -----------: | ----------: |
|  \(29_{10}\) |    **1D₁₆** |
| \(156_{10}\) |    **9C₁₆** |
| \(255_{10}\) |    **FF₁₆** |
