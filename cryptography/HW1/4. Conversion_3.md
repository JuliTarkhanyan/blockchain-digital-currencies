# Binary to Decimal and Hexadecimal Conversion

Convert:

$$
(11010110)_2
$$

to decimal and hexadecimal.

## 1. Binary to Decimal

Write the powers of 2 under each binary digit:

| Binary digit |       1 |       1 |       0 |       1 |       0 |       1 |       1 |       0 |
| ------------ | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: |
| Power of 2   | \(2^7\) | \(2^6\) | \(2^5\) | \(2^4\) | \(2^3\) | \(2^2\) | \(2^1\) | \(2^0\) |
| Value        |     128 |      64 |      32 |      16 |       8 |       4 |       2 |       1 |

Now add the values where the binary digit is **1**:

$$
128 + 64 + 16 + 4 + 2
$$

$$
= 214
$$

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(11010110)_2 = (214)_{10}}
$$

---

## 2. Binary to Hexadecimal

Split the binary number into groups of 4 bits:

$$
1101\quad1010
$$

Convert each group:

### First group: 1101

$$
1101_2 = 8+4+0+1 = 13
$$

13 in hexadecimal is **D**.

### Second group: 1010

$$
1010_2 = 8+0+2+0 = 10
$$

10 in hexadecimal is **A**.

Therefore:

$$
\boxed{(11010110)_2 = (DA)_{16}}
$$

## Final Answers

$$
\boxed{(11010110)_2 = (214)_{10} = (DA)_{16}}
$$
